Welcome Controller
You are now in command of BINGO WATCH. Your mission is to guide passengers safely through the airspace (numbers 1-90) until landing (BINGO).
The background features a monochrome OCC Tactical Radar Map (2D / 3D Globe, range rings, flight tracks and nine live weather layers: Jetstreams, Wind barbs, Cloud areas, CAT, Tropopause, Icing, Tropical cyclones, Volcanoes & SIGMET, plus RainViewer radar).

CARD GENERATOR

Print new flight plans (bingo cards) for the entire crew.

VERIFY WIN (SCANNER)

Enter Card ID to automatically validate a claimed Bingo.

PRIZE DISTRIBUTION

Manage inventory and randomly draw winners for side prizes.

LIVE WEATHER & OCC

Toggle 3D Globe, sun / day-night, range rings and the Weather Layers menu on the right toolbar.

CONFLICT RES.

Resolve "dead heat" tie-breaker situations between players.
